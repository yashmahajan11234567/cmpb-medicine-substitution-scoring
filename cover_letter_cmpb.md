I am writing to submit our manuscript entitled "Transparent Implementation and Verification of a Medicine Substitution-Scoring Algorithm: Deterministic Scoring and Unit-Consistent Dose Handling" for consideration as an original research article in Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine.

**Relevance to the journal:** This work presents an independent, deterministic Python implementation of a published medicine substitution-scoring algorithm, with full verification against all publicly labelled results. The manuscript addresses computational reproducibility in biomedical computing — a core concern of CMPB — by making a previously unpublished algorithm independently auditable and by documenting ambiguities in the published specification.

**Contribution:** The paper contributes (1) a deterministic implementation of the published DS scoring algorithm, (2) a verification protocol reproducing all 32 published Table 8 DS scores under a proposed table reconciliation, (3) documentation of unresolved unit-convention ambiguities in the published Table 7 examples, and (4) a preliminary fixed-dose-combination extension clearly labelled as unvalidated.

**Scientific qualifications:** We make no claim of clinical interchangeability or therapeutic equivalence. The proposed TRN reconciliation is inferred from published scores and has not been independently confirmed against an authoritative source. The fixed-dose-combination extension is preliminary and unvalidated against published ground truth.
